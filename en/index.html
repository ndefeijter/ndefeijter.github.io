Expertise

Approach & tools

I approach building and maintaining software as engineering. Technologies are tools: I choose what fits the problem and pick up new tools quickly.

  • The JVM platform: Java, Spring and the wider ecosystem, from new builds to modernisation.
  • Cloud: cloud-native architecture, deployment and operations.
  • CI/CD: automated building, testing and releasing.
  • Flutter: apps for mobile and web from a single codebase.
  • Observability: insight into how your systems behave, so you can steer performance and reliability proactively, before users notice anything.

About me

Niels de Feijter

I'm Niels de Feijter, a freelance software engineer since 2012, with 20 years of experience across the full software lifecycle. To me, building and maintaining software is engineering: understanding what's really needed, choosing the right tools and mastering them. I've worked in many domains: manufacturing, the energy transition, telecom, health technology and retail. Existing, complex systems are the work I enjoy most.

I'm analytical and like to get to the heart of a problem. I choose simplicity, the right tools and good collaboration. At J-Fall and BrabantJUG I keep up with what's happening in the field.
