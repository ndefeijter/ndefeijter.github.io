Privacy statement

NELIO Software Engineering (Niels de Feijter) is responsible for the processing of personal data through this website. You can reach me at info@nelio.se.

What this website doesn't do

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The fonts are hosted on this website itself, so your browser doesn't connect to other parties for them.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). On every visit GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Contact

If you email me, I use your name, email address and message only to answer your question and prepare a possible collaboration. I don't keep this data longer than needed for that. If you schedule an intro call through the booking page, Google processes your name, email address and the chosen time in Google Calendar and Google Meet.

Your rights

You can ask me which data I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email info@nelio.se to do so. If you're not satisfied with how I handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
